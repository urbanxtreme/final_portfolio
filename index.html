Feature 01NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025

Fortis Fortuna Adiuvat

Fortis Fortuna Adiuvat combines NASA's space imagery with 3D Solar System simulations to create an immersive, hands on space exploration experience.

It offers two interactive modes. The Deep Zoom NASA Image Explorer is a Google Maps style viewer for panning and zooming across gigapixel space imagery, with annotations and educational overlays. The 3D Solar System Simulation is a WebGL powered environment for exploring the planets and their orbits around the Sun.

By blending science, technology and storytelling, the project aims to make cosmic data engaging and bring the universe closer to everyone.

Feature 02National Hackathon ARCANE

Hybrid Vision Hub

Hybrid Vision Hub is an integrated workforce and machine productivity optimization platform. It tracks and analyzes the productivity of both people and machines to optimize industrial operations.

By providing real time data and predictive insights, it lets managers make informed, data driven decisions to maximize productivity, minimize downtime and improve operational efficiency.
